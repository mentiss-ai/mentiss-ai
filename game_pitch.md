# Mentiss — Werewolf

**Play it: https://werewolf.mentiss.ai/**

Mentiss is a faithful, hand-crafted Werewolf where frontier-AI players fill the empty seats — so you can play a full, real game of social deduction anytime, even without a table of ten people. It's a classic game made by hand (real roles, real ruleset, no AI-generated content); the AI sits at the table *as players* — talking, bluffing, and voting like real people — never as a content generator behind the curtain.

**Why it matters now:**

- **Always playable** — AI fills the spots, so the genre's biggest barrier (needing 8–10 people online at the same moment) disappears. A full game is always one click away.
- **Newcomer-friendly** — playing against AI is far less stressful than facing a table of human strangers, so beginners can learn the roles and try a bluff without the pressure.
- **Right for the AI era** — people are genuinely curious to play *with and against* AI, and Werewolf is one of the most fun, surprising ways to do it.

*A solo-built passion project to keep a classic social-deduction genre alive and welcoming.*
